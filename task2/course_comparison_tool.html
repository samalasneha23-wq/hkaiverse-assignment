Before using the results

The course records below are taken from the course map in this assignment repository. They are draft records, not independently verified official curriculum data. Course codes and credits must be checked against the official syllabus and regulations for the applicable admission batch before decisions are finalised. Repeated codes and inconsistent entries in the draft map should be resolved using that source.

KEEP, UPDATE, REDUCE and ADD are curriculum-audit recommendations, not official university decisions. Proposed courses are displayed separately from existing courses.

How to interpret this tool

  • Use the filters to find courses by name, code, semester or recommendation.
  • Select courses to view their combined record count and existing-course credit subtotal.
  • Export the currently visible records to CSV for further review.
  • Use the calculator to test a redesign percentage after verifying the official programme total and affected credits.
  • Review every proposed recommendation with curriculum evidence and relevant academic approval requirements before implementation.
