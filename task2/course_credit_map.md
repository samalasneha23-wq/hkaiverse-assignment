This document presents the semester-wise course structure for the B.Tech programme in Computer Science and Engineering, specialising in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science. The course map provides a foundation for evaluating curriculum relevance, identifying skill gaps, and proposing improvements based on current industry requirements.

## 9. Fourth-Year Structure

The fourth year includes a semester of academic classes and a semester dedicated to internship. The internship provides an opportunity to apply classroom knowledge to practical work environments and gain industry experience.

The course map will be used to assess foundational knowledge, technical specialisation, practical learning, and alignment with employer expectations.

## 10. Relevance to Curriculum Redesign

The courses will be evaluated against current job requirements in AI engineering, data analytics, machine learning, cloud computing, and related areas. The audit will consider both foundational subjects and specialised topics.

The resulting recommendations will identify courses to keep, update, reduce, or add, with reasons supported by curriculum content and job-market evidence.
