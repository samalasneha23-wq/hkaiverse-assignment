# Task 1: CampusFlow — Current-State Report

## 1. Overview

CampusFlow is a proposed improvement to the hostel vacating and no-dues clearance process at IFHE Hyderabad. The idea comes from my personal experience of visiting departments individually to collect signatures before vacating the hostel.

My process took approximately one to two days. This is my personal estimate, not an average measured across students.

## 2. Current Process

Based on my experience and the blank No Dues Certificate I inspected, the process involves collecting the required clearances before leaving the institution.

The form lists these clearance sections:

1. Library
2. Computer Lab
3. Academic Office
4. Chief Security Officer (CSO)
5. Student Service Department (SSD)
6. Hostel

The form also includes instructions about fee receipts, returning the identity card and room keys, updating contact details, and completing hostel handover.

## 4. Problems Observed

### Repeated department visits

I had to visit departments one by one to collect signatures. This made the process inconvenient.

### Uncertainty about outstanding clearances

When clearances are collected individually, a student may need to keep track of which sections are still pending. Whether this happens routinely needs further observation.

### Waiting and coordination

If an authorised staff member is unavailable, a student may need to return later. This is a potential issue to investigate, not a measured finding from my experience.

## 6. Improvement Opportunity

A coordinated workflow could allow students to submit one request and track the status of each required clearance. Where institutional rules permit, departments could complete their independent checks in parallel.

The proposed workflow should retain all necessary checks, clearly identify the responsible person for each step, and require final approval from an authorised official.

## 7. Expected Benefits

- Fewer unnecessary trips between offices.
- Clear visibility of pending clearances.
- Better coordination between departments.
- Easier identification of requests requiring action.
- A clearer record of completion and final approval.

These are expected benefits of the proposed design. They have not yet been measured in a live process.

## 8. Conclusion

My personal experience and inspection of the blank No Dues Certificate provide an initial basis for investigating the hostel vacating process. HostelClear proposes better coordination and status tracking while preserving required departmental clearances and authorised approval. The improvement will be evaluated using synthetic test cases and an estimate of net time saved, including review and support effort.
