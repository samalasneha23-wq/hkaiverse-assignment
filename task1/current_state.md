# Task 1: Current-State Workflow Investigation

## 1. Workflow Selected

**Workflow:** College No-Dues Clearance and Hostel Vacating

**Proposed solution name:** HostelClear

This investigation focuses on the process a student follows to complete no-dues clearance while vacating the hostel at ICFAI/IFHE. The purpose is to understand the existing steps, identify possible delays, and propose a clearer process for tracking progress.

## 2. Why This Workflow Was Selected

The workflow is relevant to students because completing clearance may involve visiting several departments and confirming that no outstanding items remain. When information is maintained separately, students may need to contact departments individually to find out what is pending.

A clearer process could help students understand which departments must be visited, what items need to be returned, and which steps remain incomplete.

## 3. Current Process: Preliminary Map

The following is a preliminary workflow based on personal recollection and inspection of a blank No-Dues Certificate. It is not a claim that every student follows exactly the same sequence.

1. A student begins the hostel-vacating and no-dues clearance process.
2. The student checks the no-dues requirements and gathers relevant documents or items.
3. The student contacts or visits the departments listed on the form.
4. Each department checks whether any dues, unreturned items, or other obligations remain.
5. If an issue is identified, the student must resolve it before clearance can be completed.
6. The relevant department confirms clearance according to its procedure.
7. The student completes any remaining hostel handover requirements.
8. The student confirms that the required clearance process is complete.

**Important:** The exact order, responsible approvers, required signatures, and final certificate-issuing authority must be verified against the current official procedure. The sequence above is a working map, not a verified official policy.

## 4. Departments and Requirements Identified

The blank No-Dues Certificate inspected for this investigation listed the following areas:

- Library
- Computer Lab
- Academic Office
- CSO
- Student Service Department (SSD)
- Hostel

The form's instructions also referred to items such as fee receipts, ID card surrender, room keys, and hostel handover.

These details describe the form inspected for this investigation. They should not be interpreted as confirmation that every item is mandatory in every case or that the form remains the latest approved version.

## 5. Handoffs and Possible Delays

| Stage | Handoff or dependency | Possible difficulty |
|---|---|---|
| Start of clearance | Student identifies required steps | Requirements may not be clear to the student |
| Department verification | Student contacts each listed department | Multiple visits may be required |
| Outstanding issue | Department informs student of a pending item | Student may need to return after resolving it |
| Clearance confirmation | Department records or confirms clearance | Student may not immediately know whether all departments have completed their checks |
| Hostel handover | Student completes applicable hostel requirements | Missing items or incomplete formalities may delay completion |
| Final confirmation | Student checks whether clearance is complete | Lack of a consolidated status may create uncertainty |

The difficulties in this table are potential issues to investigate, not confirmed incidents reported by every student.

## 6. Evidence Classification

### Observed or personally inspected

- A blank No-Dues Certificate was inspected.
- The form listed Library, Computer Lab, Academic Office, CSO, Student Service Department (SSD), and Hostel.
- The form instructions referred to fee receipts, ID card surrender, room keys, and hostel handover.
- The workflow involves checking requirements associated with multiple areas.

### Personal recollection

- Completing the process involved visiting departments individually.
- The process took approximately one to two days in my experience. This is an estimate of elapsed time for one experience, not a measured average and not the total time spent actively working on clearance.

### Assumptions requiring verification

- Students may have to make repeat visits when an item is missing or an issue remains unresolved.
- A consolidated status tracker may reduce uncertainty and unnecessary follow-up.
- Departments may be able to update a shared status if authorised by the institution.
- A designated coordinator may help students identify the next step when a department is unavailable.

- The average number of visits per student.

These unknowns should not be presented as measured facts.

## 8. Initial Problem Statement

The preliminary problem is that a student may need to coordinate clearance across several departments without a single, easy-to-check view of all pending requirements. This can create uncertainty about which step remains incomplete and whether another visit is necessary.

This is a problem hypothesis based on the workflow inspected and personal recollection. It should be validated with authorised staff or additional observations before being treated as a confirmed institution-wide problem.

## 9. Proposed Direction

HostelClear is a proposed tracking approach, not an existing ICFAI/IFHE system.

It could provide:

- A checklist of required clearance stages.
- A clear status for each stage.
- The department or authorised role responsible for the next action.
- A way to flag missing information or unresolved items.
- A final status once all required approvals are recorded.
- A manual fallback when the tracker or a department is unavailable.

Department approvals would remain with the institution's authorised staff. A tracker must not independently approve dues clearance, bypass official rules, or expose students' personal information.

## 10. Privacy and Limitations

This report does not include student records, fee details, identification numbers, room numbers, or personal clearance documents. No claim of faculty interviews, staff approval, or institution-wide measurement is made here.

Before publishing any official form or collecting additional information, permission and applicable institutional rules should be checked. If permission to share a document has not been confirmed, the document should not be uploaded publicly.

## 11. Conclusion

The preliminary investigation identifies a multi-department no-dues and hostel-vacating workflow that may benefit from clearer status tracking. The current-state map separates personally inspected information from recollection, assumptions, and unknowns. The proposed HostelClear approach will be evaluated through synthetic test cases and illustrative time calculations rather than unverified claims about a live system.
