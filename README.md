## Task 1: HostelClear

**A Proposed Streamlined Hostel Vacating and No-Dues Clearance Workflow**

HostelClear explores how the hostel vacating clearance process could be better coordinated to reduce repeated department visits and delays caused by staff availability, while preserving required checks and authorised approvals.

### Files

- [`Evidence Log`](task1/evidence_log.md) — documents personal recollections, form-based evidence, estimates and assumptions.
- [`Current-State Report`](task1/current_state.md) — describes the existing process as initially reconstructed from my experience and the blank no-dues form.
- [`Proposed Workflow`](task1/proposed_workflow.md) — outlines a coordinated clearance process with status tracking and exception handling.
- [`Synthetic Test Cases`](task1/test_cases.md) — describes five simulated scenarios and their expected outcomes.
- [`Time-Savings Estimate`](task1/time_savings.md) — explains the illustrative calculation and how actual savings should be measured.

### Evidence and Limitations

The initial investigation uses my personal experience and the blank IFHE No Dues Certificate. The approximately one-to-two-day duration is my recollection, not a measured average. Illustrative time-saving figures are assumptions, not verified results.

HostelClear is a proposed solution, not an existing institutional system. The required checks and approval responsibilities must be preserved and confirmed before implementation.
