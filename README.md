# HKAIverse Round 2 Assessment

## Project Overview

This repository contains the two assignments prepared for the HKAIverse Round 2 assessment:

1. **Task 1 – College Workflow Investigation**
2. **Task 2 – Curriculum Audit and Redesign**

The project investigates a college clearance workflow, proposes practical improvements, reviews curriculum relevance, and identifies opportunities to strengthen practical learning.

---

## Task 1: College Workflow Investigation

### Objective

Investigate a college clearance process, document its steps and handoffs, identify difficulties, and propose an improved workflow with clear responsibilities and exception handling.

### Process Studied

The investigation focuses on the college no-dues clearance process, which involves obtaining confirmations from relevant departments.

### Proposed Improvement: HostelClear

HostelClear is a proposed workflow-management solution for tracking clearance requests. It aims to improve visibility of pending actions and clarify responsibilities while preserving departmental approval authority.

The investigation covers:

- Current process steps, handoffs, approvals, and exceptions.
- An evidence log distinguishing observations, estimates, and assumptions.
- The identified problem and its estimated scale.
- Proposed inputs, statuses, accountable ownership, approval boundaries, and fallback arrangements.
- Five synthetic test cases: normal submission, missing information, duplicate request, unauthorised request, and failed step.
- Expected recovery actions and an illustrative net time-saving calculation that includes review and support effort.

## Task 2: Curriculum Audit and Redesign

### Objective

Review the curriculum for the applicable programme, branch, admission batch, and curriculum version. Identify opportunities to strengthen practical learning and industry relevance while considering credit totals, prerequisites, and academic regulations.

### Scope of the Audit

The curriculum review covers:

- Course and credit mapping by semester.
- KEEP, UPDATE, REDUCE, and ADD recommendations.
- Specific reasons supporting curriculum decisions.
- Comparison with at least three real, dated job descriptions.
- Proposed changes affecting approximately 20–25% of programme credits, subject to verified credit totals and academic requirements.
- Five AI-assisted practical exercises across five subjects.
- AI prompts, model details, dates, outputs, and verification records.
- Faculty or graduate feedback, where accessible, or an explanation of access limitations.
- An interactive course comparison and credit calculator.
- A proposed practical lab or capstone based on the workflow investigated in Task 1.

## Limitations

- The proposed workflow has not been deployed or approved by the institution.
- Synthetic test cases describe expected behaviour; they do not demonstrate testing of a live system.
- Time-saving estimates are illustrative unless supported by measured observations.
- Curriculum and credit calculations depend on the applicable official syllabus and regulations.
- Feedback is reported only if it was actually obtained; otherwise, access limitations are explained.
- Proposed curriculum changes remain subject to academic approval and prerequisite requirements.
